01About Dav-Ric

The real estate arm of Dav-Ric Group

A good home changes how life feels.

Dav-Ric Homes brings care and discipline to every part of the development journey — from the first idea and land acquisition to construction, marketing and homeowner support.

Our work grows from a Group founded in 2008 and guided by competence, resourcefulness and a commitment to surpass expectations. The five-project portfolio spans residential communities and the completed Dav-Ric Towers headquarters across Ibadan and Abuja.
